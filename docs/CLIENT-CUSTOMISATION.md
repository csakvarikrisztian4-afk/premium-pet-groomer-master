# Client customisation checklist

1. Edit every field in `assets/js/site-config.js`.
2. Add the verified business name, phone, email, opening hours and exact address.
3. Add the real booking destination or keep the contact form flow.
4. Add the exact Google Maps search and embed URLs. Never use a generic query that displays competitors.
5. Replace the hero, about and gallery images with client-owned, permissioned photographs where possible.
6. Replace all three neutral review placeholders with real, permissioned reviews.
7. Confirm the services, FAQ answers and Hungarian copy with the owner.
8. Complete the privacy notice, imprint, tax number and registration details.
9. Test call, WhatsApp, booking, review and Maps links on mobile.
10. Review the page at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px before launch.
