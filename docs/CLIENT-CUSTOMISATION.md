# Client customisation checklist

1. Update every value in `assets/js/site-config.js`.
2. Replace stock images with licensed or client-approved local images.
3. Replace placeholder reviews with permissioned, accurate reviews.
4. Verify services, opening hours, phone, address, map and booking links.
5. Complete the legal name, address, registration number and tax number.
6. Replace the sample privacy text with the business's actual notice.
7. Test the form destination and booking flow.
8. Test at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px.
9. Confirm there is no horizontal overflow and every CTA works.
10. Remove the concept banner before production launch.
