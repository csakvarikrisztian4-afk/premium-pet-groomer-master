# Image guide

Recommended source files:

- Hero: portrait or near-square, at least 1200 px wide.
- About: landscape 4:3, at least 1200 × 900 px.
- Gallery: consistent 4:3 crops, at least 900 × 675 px.

Prefer WebP or AVIF for client photos where browser support and workflow permit. Keep a JPEG fallback only when necessary. Aim for roughly 100–250 KB per gallery image and under 400 KB for the hero image without visible quality loss.
